# E-Commerce Sales Data Analysis

## Overview

This project performs Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on an E-Commerce Sales dataset to uncover valuable business insights related to sales performance, customer purchasing behavior, product demand, and revenue trends.

Using Python data analysis libraries, the project identifies patterns in sales across different months, cities, product categories, and time periods to support data-driven business decisions.

---

## Problem Statement

E-commerce businesses generate large amounts of transactional data every day. Analyzing this data helps organizations understand customer behavior, optimize inventory management, and improve sales strategies.

The objective of this project is to analyze sales data and answer key business questions regarding revenue generation, product demand, and purchasing trends.

## Key Insights

### Revenue Trends

* Revenue gradually increases during the early months of the year.
* A decline is observed during the middle months.
* Sales recover significantly and reach their highest level in December.

### Product Demand

* Batteries and charging cables are among the most frequently purchased products.
* Home appliances show comparatively lower demand.

### Customer Behavior

* Customer purchases increase throughout the day.
* Night-time generates the highest revenue.
* Morning contributes the least revenue.

### Sales Performance

* Higher quantities ordered generally correspond to higher sales values.
* Certain product categories consistently outperform others.
